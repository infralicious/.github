# Code of conduct

This file is the org default, for repos without their own code.
A repo code, if it exists, wins over this file.

## Our pledge

We welcome all people, regardless of background or identity.
We work in public, with respect, patience, and clear words.
We focus on the work, not on the person, in reviews.

## Expected behavior

Use welcoming language, and respect different views.
Accept feedback gracefully, and give feedback kindly.
Show empathy, for users, contributors, and maintainers.
Report problems early, with facts, not with blame.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, insults, or personal attacks are not allowed.
Sexualized language, or unwanted sexual attention, is not allowed.
Publishing private information, without consent, is not allowed.
Disruption, trolling, or bad faith actions are not allowed.

## Enforcement

A maintainer may warn, hide, or remove content that breaks this code.
A maintainer may ban a person, for repeated or severe breaks.
Report a break, with the contact form, at https://infralicious.com/contact.html
Reports stay private, to the extent the law allows.
No retaliation is allowed, for a good faith report.

## Scope

This code applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and chats.
It applies to public spaces, where Infralicious work happens.
It does not apply to private life, outside Infralicious work.
